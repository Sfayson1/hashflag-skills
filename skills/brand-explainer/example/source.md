# Fix-It Saturday comes to Alder Bay

> Fictional. Written as example input for the brand-explainer skill. Alder Bay is the fictional town from `fixtures/`.

Alder Bay Public Library held its first Fix-It Saturday on September 12, 2026. Neighbors brought things that were broken. Volunteers helped them fix those things on the spot, for free, while the owner did the work and learned how.

## How it works

You check in at the front desk and say what is wrong. Staff send you to one of four repair tables: bikes, lamps and small electronics, clothing, and furniture. A volunteer looks at the item with you. You hold the tools while they explain each step. If a part is missing, you leave with a list of exactly what to buy. Nobody fixes it for you; you fix it together.

## The numbers

From the library's sign-in sheet: 64 people came, and they brought 91 items. By closing time, 58 of those items worked again.

From the volunteer roster: 14 volunteers worked the four tables.
