## Bike repair that gets you back on the road

> Fictional business. Created as test content for hashflag-skills. Not a real company.

Lark & Lug is a small repair shop in Alder Bay. We fix road bikes, commuters, kids' bikes, cargo bikes, and that rusty thing in your garage you swear you'll ride again. Two mechanics. One workbench each. No upselling.

## Services

### Tune-up

The basics, done right. We adjust your gears and brakes, true your wheels (that means straightening the wobble), check the chain, and tighten everything that's loose. Most bikes are ready the same day.

### Overhaul

For bikes that need more than a tune-up. We take the whole thing apart, clean every part, replace what's worn out, and put it back together. Plan on leaving it with us for a few days.

### Flat fix

Bring it in, we patch or replace the tube while you wait. If the tire is worn through, we'll show you before we touch anything.

### Brake service

Squeaky, soft, or just not stopping like it used to. We replace pads, adjust cables, and bleed hydraulic brakes (that means pushing out the air bubbles so they feel firm again).

### Wheel building and truing

Bent rim? Broken spoke? We can fix it or build you a new wheel from scratch.

### E-bike and cargo bike repair

We work on the bike parts of e-bikes: brakes, gears, wheels, and tires. We don't open up motors or batteries. If that's the problem, we'll tell you who can help.

### Bike fit

We adjust your seat height, handlebar reach, and pedal position so riding stops hurting your knees, back, or wrists. Takes about an hour.

## FAQ

### Do I need an appointment?

For flat fixes, no. Just come in. For tune-ups, overhauls, and bike fits, Book online or Call us so we can plan the day.

### How long will my bike be in the shop?

Tune-ups are usually done the same day if you drop off before noon. Overhauls take a few days. Wheel builds depend on parts. We'll give you a pickup day when you drop off.

### How much does a repair cost?

Every bike is different, so we don't list set rates. Call for a quote. We'll look at your bike and tell you exactly what it needs before we start any work.

### Do you sell bikes?

No. We're a repair shop. We do carry tubes, tires, brake pads, chains, and other parts we use on repairs.

### Can you fix my e-bike?

Yes for brakes, gears, wheels, and tires. No for motors, batteries, and wiring. We'll point you to someone who handles those.

### Will you use parts I bought online?

Usually, yes. Bring them in and we'll check that they fit your bike first. If they don't, we'll let you know before we start.

### What if my bike is really old?

We love old bikes! Steel frames from decades ago can ride great with a little work. If a part is hard to find, we'll tell you upfront.
