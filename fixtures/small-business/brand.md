## Voice

Blunt, playful, nerdy, quick.

Example sentences:

- "Squeaky brakes? Bring it in. We'll have it quiet by lunch."
- "We fix the bike you have, not the one we want to sell you."

## Copy rules

1. Calls to action are one literal verb: Book, Call.
2. Say "riders," never "customers."
3. Never put a price in writing. Point people to call for a quote.
4. Use bike terms, then explain them once in plain words.
5. One exclamation point per page, max.
