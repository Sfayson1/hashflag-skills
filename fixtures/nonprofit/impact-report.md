# Our Year Together

> Fictional organization. Created as test content for hashflag-skills. Not a real nonprofit.

This was a big year for Wrenfield Community Pantry. More neighbors came through our doors. More volunteers showed up to meet them. And our garden finally grew enough to fill a table every Saturday.

Here is what we did together.

## Groceries for families

Our Saturday grocery pickup is still the heart of what we do.

- We served **310 unique families** this year, from our intake log.
- Those families made **2,080 grocery pickups**, from our Saturday sign-in sheets.
- We handed out **96,000 pounds of food**, from our warehouse scale records.

Most families come every week. Some come once and never need us again. Both are wins.

## Weekend snack bags for kids

School lunch stops on Friday. Hunger doesn't. In Alder Bay, **1 in 4 kids skips meals on weekends**. That's why we started packing snack bags.

- **75 kids** got a snack bag every Friday, from our school partner list.
- Volunteers packed **3,600 snack bags** over the school year, from our packing tally sheet.

Each bag has two breakfasts, two lunches, and a few snacks. Teachers slip them into backpacks so no one has to ask.

## The community garden

The garden started as a few raised beds behind the pantry. Now it feeds people.

- We harvested **2,300 pounds of produce**, from our garden harvest log.

Tomatoes, greens, squash, and herbs went straight to the Saturday table. Neighbors who helped plant got first pick.

## The people who made it happen

None of this works without volunteers.

- **85 active volunteers** gave their time this year, from our volunteer roster.
- Together they logged **4,200 volunteer hours**, from our volunteer shift log.

They sorted cans. They packed bags. They weeded the garden in July heat. They learned names and remembered them.

## The money

Every dollar went to food, the building, or the people who keep it running.

- We raised **$142,000** this year, from our year-end bookkeeping.

Most of it came from neighbors giving small amounts every month. That steady support is what lets us plan ahead.

## What's next

Next year we want to add a second pickup day for families who work Saturdays. We need more volunteers and more monthly donors to get there.

If you can give, Donate. If you can show up, Volunteer.

Thank you for being a good neighbor.

The Wrenfield team
